Arrival board

First things to make easier

This is a board of practical items that tend to matter in the first days: access, accounts, paperwork, transport, food, and one human being who knows where the printer lives.

Useful principle

Make a folder, save PDFs, keep screenshots, and ask early. The first week is mostly practical logistics that become easier once written down.

Checklist

Arrival board

The boxes save in your browser only.

  • Tell the department you arrived. Ask about office access, keys, mailbox, mailing lists, and local routines.
  • Set up your FU account. It unlocks my.FU, email, eduroam, Campus Management, library systems, and other university services.
  • Install transport tools. BVG, S-Bahn Berlin, and DB Navigator cover most situations. Students: check the digital semester ticket setup.
  • Prepare Anmeldung. Once you move into a Berlin flat, residence registration is normally due within 14 days. Keep the landlord confirmation.
  • Confirm health insurance status. Students need enrollment-compatible proof; employees should coordinate with HR/payroll.
  • Check your visa/residence timeline. If you need the LEA, collect PDFs early and use the official online route for your case.
  • Create and validate your Campuscard. It is your student ID, library card, and Mensa payment card, but not your transport ticket.
  • Add department rhythms to your calendar. Research seminar: Thursdays 16:15–17:45 during term time. Joint lunch: Wednesdays 13:00 every week.
  • Complete fire-safety onboarding in the first week. Read the current internal material, pass the official quiz, and archive the result as instructed.
  • Set up library access. Confirm Campuscard or employee library card, test Primo login, and note the interlibrary loan route.
  • Locate food anchors. Mensa, Aldi/Lidl, Rewe/Edeka, dm/Rossmann, a Späti, and one dependable nearby lunch option.
Before arrival

Make the PDF folder

Save admission, employment or invitation letters, funding proof, health insurance, passport/visa scans, and housing documents.

Day one

Find your anchors

Pin Lansstraße 7–9, your route to campus, your nearest grocery store, a pharmacy, and one calm place to sit.

First contacts

Ask normal questions

Where do people print? Which mailing list matters? How does the office key work? What is the room-booking norm?

Do not postpone

Residence and insurance

Anything involving address registration, residence status, payroll, enrollment, or health insurance deserves early attention.
