Berlin administration

Address registration, residence status, health insurance, bank account, tax ID, and the humble PDF: not exciting, but extremely useful when done early.

Friendly warning

Rules change. Use the links here as your starting point and verify the exact current requirements before submitting anything.

Payroll only

Business travel and the A1 certificate

Before booking business travel, contact the department office at sociology@jfki.fu-berlin.de. For official business trips, use the current faculty travel route. For work trips to EU/EEA countries or Switzerland, employees normally need an A1 certificate to document that German social-insurance rules continue to apply during the trip. Start early; the certificate is paperwork, not a souvenir.

Address registration

Anmeldung

Berlin expects residents to register their address after moving in. The usual deadline is 14 days. You will need identification and the landlord’s move-in confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung). The registration certificate often unlocks bank, tax, residence, and contract procedures.

Residence status

LEA / Ausländeramt

Berlin’s immigration office is the LEA. Many cases now start online; appointments are usually assigned after documents are checked. Students and researchers are handled through specific units/services, so choose the category carefully.

Student residence information currently advises submitting extension applications preferably eight weeks before expiry. Researchers and employees should coordinate with FU Welcome Service or HR.

Files that help

Your PDF folder

Create one folder named something like Berlin admin 2026. Keep scans of passport, visa, admission/employment letter, funding proof, rental documents, health insurance, Anmeldung, tax ID, and every LEA confirmation.

Tip: upload one clearly named PDF per document when forms ask for uploads. Clear files reduce back-and-forth.

Health, money, tax

The triangle: insurance → bank → tax ID

Health insurance

Students need valid proof for enrollment. Employees should check payroll-compatible coverage with HR.

Bank account

Many banks want Anmeldung; some digital banks are more flexible. Keep your full legal name consistent.

Tax ID

Usually arrives by post after registration. Photograph the letter and never confuse it with your tax number.
