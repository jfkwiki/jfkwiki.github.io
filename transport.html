Zones

AB, BC, ABC

Berlin A is the inner city up to the S-Bahn ring; B extends to the city boundary; C covers the surrounding area, including BER airport and Potsdam. Most city travel is AB. Airport or Potsdam? Usually ABC.

Tickets

Pick the right ticket

Paper tickets usually need validation before travel; app tickets do not. Short trips are cheaper but strict. Monthly and subscription tickets can be worthwhile if you commute.

Multi-month stays

Deutschlandticket

For stays of several months, the Deutschlandticket is usually the simplest public-transport option once you have a German address/payment setup. DB currently lists it as a monthly subscription for nationwide local and regional public transport. It is not valid on ICE, IC, or EC long-distance trains.

Students

Semester ticket ≠ Campuscard

FU’s Campuscard no longer functions as the semester transport ticket. Students receive the Deutschlandsemesterticket digitally, currently via the S-Bahn Berlin app, and must show official photo ID during inspections.

Staff

Deutschlandticket Job / FU jobticket

FU employees may be eligible for a subsidized jobticket, including the Deutschlandticket Job. Ask the department office for the local route, or use FU’s jobticket information and HR contact if you are already in the payroll system.

Practice

Make everyone’s trip easier

Let people exit first. Move away from doors. Do not block the yellow bus-door zone. Night buses replace many weekday U-Bahn routes; on weekends, the subway runs through the night. Rush hours are roughly morning and late afternoon; your backpack deserves spatial awareness.
