Emergency contacts, escape routes, assembly points, fire-safety assistants, and the local training material that should be easy to find before anyone needs it.

First-week task

Read the current internal material, complete the official quiz correctly, and archive the result as instructed.

In an emergency: call 112 for fire, ambulance, and immediate danger. On campus, FU also lists the central technical emergency control room at +49 30 838-55555.
First week

Complete the fire-safety onboarding

In the first week, read the current department fire-safety material and complete the official quiz or Blackboard check correctly. Save the quiz result as a screenshot, PDF, or exported confirmation in the onboarding folder, or send it to the department office if that is the current local procedure.

If you cannot access the quiz yet, ask the department office or your host which current onboarding route to use.

Archive

Save the result

File the completion evidence with your onboarding documents. A useful filename includes your name, the date, and “Brandschutz” so the result can be found later without archaeological work.

Building basics

What everyone should know

Learn the escape route from your office or seminar room, the nearest fire alarm/manual call point, the nearest extinguisher, and the assembly point. The green evacuation signs provide the official route information for leaving the building safely.

Alarm practice

If the alarm sounds

Leave the building by the marked route, help people nearby if safe, do not use elevators, go to the assembly point, and wait until the building is released. Do not re-enter until authorized.

Calling it in

Report clearly

Give the exact location, what happened, how many people are affected, who is calling, and wait for questions. A good emergency call is short and concrete.
