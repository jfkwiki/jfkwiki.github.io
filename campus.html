FU & JFKI

Your Dahlem home base

The JFKI sits in Dahlem’s research landscape: libraries, seminars, archives, quiet streets, and a useful concentration of people who know which office handles which question.

Address

Freie Universität Berlin, John F. Kennedy Institute for North American Studies, Department of Sociology, Lansstraße 7–9, 14195 Berlin.

Department rhythm

Academic life

During term time, the sociology research seminar meets on Thursdays, 16:15–17:45. The joint department lunch happens every Wednesday at 13:00, including semester breaks.

Doctoral researchers

Dahlem Research School

DRS is FU’s central contact point for doctoral researchers. Use it for qualification courses, advice, funding information, and the “how does a doctorate work here?” questions that nobody should have to reverse-engineer alone.

FU: international doctoral researchers
Visiting scholars

FU Welcome Center

Visiting scholars and scientists should register with FU’s Welcome Services. They assist with common pre-arrival and during-stay questions around visas, authorities, and settling in.

Campus stack

The admin platforms you will actually use

FU account

Your central login for FU services, from email to my.FU and campus systems.

eduroam

Campus Wi-Fi and, once configured, Wi-Fi at many universities worldwide using your FU account.

Campus Management

Course, module, exam, certificate, and student-administration workflows live here.

Campuscard

Student ID, library card, locker and Mensa payment card. Validate it each semester.

Library

Students and doctoral candidates use the Campuscard; employees create a library card through my.FU.

Mensa

A practical lunch option and a reliable place to learn campus rhythms.

Near campus

Dahlem micro-orientation

Useful anchors: U3 Freie Universität/Thielplatz, U3 Dahlem-Dorf, the Philological Library, Mensa II, Botanischer Garten, Domäne Dahlem, Grunewald, Schlachtensee. The campus is less “city grid” and more “academic garden district,” so pin locations before your first meeting.
