FU & JFKI

Your Dahlem home base

The JFKI sits in Dahlem’s research landscape: libraries, seminars, archives, course schedules, institute events, and a useful concentration of people who know which office handles which question.

Address

Freie Universität Berlin, John F. Kennedy Institute for North American Studies, Department of Sociology, Lansstraße 7–9, 14195 Berlin.

Department rhythm

Academic life

During term time, the sociology research seminar meets on Thursdays, 16:15–17:45. The joint department lunch happens every Wednesday at 13:00, including semester breaks.

Department

Sociology at JFKI

Public department contact: sociology@jfki.fu-berlin.de. The official page lists the front office and current team. For keys, room access, office routines, mailing lists, and guest-account routing, start with the department office.

FU account

Staff accounts and guest accounts

You need an FU account for eduroam, FU email/webmail, library-related services, literature search, Webex, e-learning tools, and many internal services. Staff members normally receive account activation information automatically. Guests can request a guest account using the German Antragsformular für Gast-Accounts; fill it in and coordinate signature/submission with the department office or host.

Background viewing

Freie Universität Berlin on film

The 27-minute ARTE/NDR documentary Universities of the World: Freie Universität Berlin introduces the university through its 1948 founding, Dahlem campus architecture, Cold War setting, protest history, and present-day international profile. It is a useful visual introduction to the institution behind the practical campus information on this page.

ARTE lists the stream as available until 27 January 2027. The current player offers German audio, German closed captions, and French audio; an official English-subtitled or YouTube version was not available when this link was checked on 31 October 2026.

Doctoral researchers

Dahlem Research School

DRS is FU’s central contact point for doctoral researchers. Use it for qualification courses, advice, funding information, and the “how does a doctorate work here?” questions that nobody should have to reverse-engineer alone.

Campus stack

The admin platforms you will actually use

FU account

Your central login for FU services, from email to my.FU, library tools, VPN, Webex, and campus systems.

eduroam

Campus Wi-Fi and, once configured, Wi-Fi at many universities worldwide using your FU account.

Campus Management

Course, module, exam, certificate, and student-administration workflows live here.

Campuscard

Student ID, library card, locker and Mensa payment card. Validate it each semester.

Library

Students and doctoral candidates use the Campuscard; employees create a library card through my.FU.

Mensa

A practical lunch option and a reliable place to learn campus rhythms.

Near campus

Dahlem micro-orientation

Useful anchors: U3 Freie Universität/Thielplatz, U3 Dahlem-Dorf, the Philological Library, Mensa II, Botanischer Garten, Domäne Dahlem, Grunewald, Schlachtensee. The campus is less “city grid” and more “academic garden district,” so pin locations before your first meeting.
