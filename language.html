Language learning

German learning routes

Institutional and course-based options for people who want German to become part of their Berlin toolkit: FU courses, Language Center routes, placement requirements, Weiterbildung, and intensive language schools.

Practical point

Check eligibility and registration windows early. Language courses are most useful when they are planned before the semester fills up.

FU courses

German language courses

FU offers German courses for certain international students and exchange routes. These courses often run through the semester rather than as daily intensive courses. Eligibility, placement, registration deadlines, and course formats vary, so check the official page for your status.

FU German language courses
Language Center

Sprachenzentrum

FU’s Language Center offers structured language-learning options. Placement procedures and registration details matter; do not assume that every course is open to every status group.

FU Language Center
Other FU courses

Weiterbildungszentrum

FU’s Weiterbildungszentrum lists professional-development courses, short-notice course offers, and other training opportunities. Some offers are particularly relevant for staff members and may be free or reduced depending on status.

FU Weiterbildungszentrum
Intensive German

Commercial language schools

If you want faster language progress than a semester-long weekly course can provide, compare private intensive courses. Check location, schedule, group size, cancellation rules, and whether the school’s level placement matches your actual level.
