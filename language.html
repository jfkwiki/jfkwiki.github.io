Language learning

German learning routes

Institutional and self-study options for people who want German to become part of their Berlin toolkit: FU courses, Language Center routes, placement requirements, and public learning resources.

Practical point

Check eligibility and registration windows early. Language courses are most useful when they are planned before the semester fills up.

FU courses

German language courses

FU offers German courses for certain international students and exchange routes. Eligibility, placement, registration deadlines, and course formats vary, so check the official page for your status.

Language Center

Sprachenzentrum

FU’s Language Center offers structured language-learning options. Placement procedures and registration details matter; do not assume that every course is open to every status group.

Public resources

Goethe-Institut materials are useful for structured practice. Easy German is especially good for everyday listening with subtitles and natural speech. Use these as supplements, not as a replacement for a routine.
