Department rhythm

Academic life at JFKI Sociology

The department is not only offices and paperwork. It has a weekly rhythm: term-time seminars, Wednesday lunches, workshops, guest speakers, and the ordinary conversations where a lot of academic life actually happens.

Quick calendar

Research seminar: Thursdays, 16:15–17:45, during term time.

Joint lunch: Wednesdays, 13:00, every week.

Term times

Plan your stay around teaching weeks

Academic activity is densest during lecture periods: broadly mid-October to mid-February and mid-April to mid-July. Exact dates vary by academic year, so check the FU academic calendar before booking travel or planning research visits.

Example orientation: FU lists Summer Semester 2026 lectures from 13 April to 18 July 2026 and Winter Semester 2026/27 lectures from 12 October 2026 to 13 February 2027.

FU academic calendar
Mandatory during term

Research seminar

During term time, the department has an ongoing research seminar every Thursday, 16:15–17:45. Incoming PhD students, visiting scholars, and longer-term guests should treat it as part of their stay.

Ask your host or the department office for the current room, mailing list, and calendar invitation.

Weekly

Joint lunch

Every Wednesday at 13:00, regardless of term time, the department has a joint lunch. It is one of the easiest ways to meet people, hear what is happening, and become part of the everyday department rhythm.

Extra events

Workshops and guests

The department also organizes workshops, guest speakers, and other academic activities, often one or two larger events in a term. If you are in Berlin, assume attendance is expected unless your host says otherwise.
