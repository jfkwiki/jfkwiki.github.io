Intellectual Berlin

A spatial map for academic wandering

Berlin’s intellectual life is not in one center. It runs west–east, campus–archive, seminar–library, policy–culture. Use BVG for routes; use this map for orientation.

It is schematic rather than cartographic: a practical mental map of institutions worth knowing.
