Housing

Housing routes for incoming scholars

A practical frame for incoming scholars: university housing routes, researcher housing, furnished temporary options, private portals, and what to ask the department before arrival.

Housing moves quickly: treat every offer carefully, do not transfer large deposits without a contract and verification, and keep documents ready. A polite, complete application is usually more effective than a long personal message.
Internal lead

Department housing leads

Ask the department office or host whether there is current internal housing information for incoming scholars. Availability changes quickly, so treat any local information as a starting point rather than a guarantee.

Best use: treat it as a warm-start list, not as guaranteed availability.

Longer stays

Public and large-provider housing routes

For longer stays, do not rely only on highly commercial portals. They can be useful, but furnished short-term listings are often expensive. Also try communal and large-provider routes with many regular apartments. These sites are often German-language, but concise inquiries in English are normal.

Researcher housing

Academic and temporary routes

Researcher-oriented housing can be more realistic than searching for a long-term unfurnished flat from abroad. For stays of a few months, check the IBZ Berlin; it specializes in scholar housing and may offer rooms with communal facilities.

Graduate students

Studierendenwerk Berlin

Graduate students should also check Studierendenwerk Berlin. It runs student residences across the city and provides a housing portal for applications. Availability is limited, so apply early and keep other routes open.

Temporary furnished

Arrive first, search better

A furnished short-term place can be expensive but reduces stress. It also gives you a Berlin address, local viewing flexibility, and time to learn which commute you can tolerate.

Private portals

Commercial portals: useful, crowded, uneven

ImmoScout, Immowelt, WG-Gesucht, and furnished-apartment sites can still help, especially for temporary arrival housing. For long-term apartments, treat them as one channel among several and watch for inflated furnished short-term prices. Prepare a compact application packet: identity, funding/employment proof, enrollment/admission or invitation, SCHUFA if available, and a short message that presents you as a reliable future tenant.

Dahlem logic

Commute thinking

Near FU is pleasant but not mandatory. U3 access matters; S-Bahn plus U-Bahn can work; bikes change the map. Test the route at the time of day you will actually use it.

Paperwork

Housing connects to Anmeldung

For address registration, you normally need a landlord move-in confirmation. Confirm before signing that Anmeldung is possible at the address.
