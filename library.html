JFKI library FAQ
Library card

Campuscard or employee card

Students and doctoral candidates normally use the Campuscard as their library card. FU employees create a library card through my.FU; after the library system synchronizes the account, Primo login should work.

Search

Primo

Use Primo to search FU holdings, request books, renew loans, and manage your account. For many questions, “check Primo first” is the boring but correct answer.

Loans and renewals

Loan periods and renewal conditions depend on status and item type. The JFKI FAQ gives practical rules for borrowing, renewals, lockers, reading rooms, and local library services.

When FU does not have it

Interlibrary loan

If an item is not available in FU libraries, order it through interlibrary loan via Primo/KOBV. FU notes that staff interlibrary loans are free; students usually pay a small fee. Pickup and return are handled through the Central Library.
