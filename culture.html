Culture, leisure, city life

How to enjoy Berlin before it becomes your errands

Berlin rewards people who wander. Pick one official highlight, one neighbourhood, and one small surprise each week.

Basic orientation

Use official calendars and local recommendations together. Berlin is large enough that no single list will cover it well.

Culture calendar

What’s on?

visitBerlin and Berlin.de maintain event calendars for concerts, museums, festivals, exhibitions, readings, cinema, theatre, and seasonal formats. Search by date; Berlin is too large to “just know.”

Low-budget joy

Free and cheap Berlin

Look for free exhibitions, open-air festivals, public talks, gallery weekends, lakes, parks, memorials, and neighbourhood walks. Berlin is generous if you are willing to move slowly.

Museums

Start with the obvious, then defect

Museum Island, Jewish Museum, Neue Nationalgalerie, Hamburger Bahnhof, Berlinische Galerie, Museum für Naturkunde, Deutsches Technikmuseum, and the city’s smaller special-interest museums can each justify a weekend.
