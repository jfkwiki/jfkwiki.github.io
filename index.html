Welcome to Berlin, Dahlem, and the art of getting oriented.

A practical wiki for incoming doctoral researchers, visiting scholars, students, and new colleagues: where to click, what to book, how to ride the U-Bahn, what to ask the department, and how basic everyday systems such as seminars, libraries, deposits, housing, and Sunday closures work.

What this wiki does

It collects the first practical questions in one place: Berlin registration, LEA, FU systems, academic life, the department, public transport, language, shopping, food, libraries, leisure, and intellectual life across the city.

It is not a substitute for official rules. It is a practical index to help newcomers find the right page, office, or link faster.

Berlin orientation quiz

Five questions, no shame. If you miss one, Berlin will provide a practical demonstration soon enough.

1. You land at BER and travel to Dahlem. Which ticket zone usually matters?
2. You move into a Berlin flat. Which document should you guard carefully?
3. Your Campuscard has arrived. Can it be used as your public transport ticket?
4. A seminar is listed as 16:00 c.t. What should you assume?
5. It is Sunday evening and your fridge is empty. What is the most realistic plan?

Important: immigration, enrollment, employment, health insurance, fees, and ticket rules change. Use this wiki as orientation, then verify details with Berlin, BVG, FU, and JFKI sources before acting.
